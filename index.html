Get set up in three steps

  1. Install Expo Go

    It’s free. If you already have it, update it, because Classic Hub needs the latest version.

    App Store Google Play
  2. Scan the code

    iPhone: open the Camera app and tap the banner that appears. Android: open Expo Go and tap “Scan QR code”. Reading this on your phone? Tap “Open in Expo Go” above instead.

  3. Sign in with any Ghana number

    No real text message is sent. When asked for the code, enter 123456. You can also tap “Look around first” to skip signing in.

Things to try

Order dinner

Pick a restaurant, add food, check out and watch the courier on the map. Try promo code CLASSIC20.

Book a caterer

Open the Book tab, choose an occasion and send a request. The vendor confirms within seconds, then you pay a deposit.

Run a restaurant

Account, then “Switch to vendor mode”. Answer booking requests, mark dishes sold out and see your sales.

Order with friends

On any restaurant page tap “Order with friends”. Pretend friends join and the basket shows who owes what.

Good to know

  • All payments are pretend. No money moves, even when you choose MoMo or card.
  • Restaurants, people and prices are made up for testing.
  • Your orders and settings stay on your phone only.
  • New versions arrive by themselves the next time you open the app.
  • See “Project is incompatible with this version of Expo Go”? Update Expo Go from your app store.
